## Assignments

<div class="publication">
<span class="pub-tag">Course research · 2026 · EMET8002</span>
<h3>Do Short-Term Unconditional Cash Transfers Reduce Reliance on External Borrowing? Evidence from Indonesia</h3>
<p class="item-meta">Case Studies in Applied Economics and Econometrics, Australian National University</p>
<p>Replicates and extends an evaluation of Indonesian cash transfers using IFLS data and coarsened exact matching with difference-in-differences. Finds an increase of 4.5 percentage points in attempted external borrowing and 5.0 percentage points in loan receipt among BLSM recipients.</p>
<p class="pub-actions"><a href="/assets/files/research/cash-transfers-borrowing.pdf">PDF</a></p>
</div>

<div class="publication">
<span class="pub-tag">Course research · 2026 · ECON8001</span>
<h3>Labour Mobility, Competing Auctions, and the Labor Share: A Comment on Mangin (2017)</h3>
<p class="item-meta">Topics in Macroeconomics, Australian National University</p>
<p>Develops a two-region extension of a competing-auctions labour market model, studying when decentralised migration is efficient and how mobility affects the aggregate labour share.</p>
<p class="pub-actions"><a href="/assets/files/research/labour-mobility-mangin.pdf">PDF</a></p>
</div>

<div class="publication">
<span class="pub-tag">Course research · 2025 · IDEC8010</span>
<h3>Do Aggregate Trade Restrictions Affect Sectors Equally? Evidence from High- and Low-GVC Industries Using a New Measure of Trade Restrictiveness</h3>
<p class="item-meta">Quantitative International Economics, Australian National University</p>
<p>Uses a gravity framework and PPML estimation with sectoral trade data to investigate whether trade restrictions have heterogeneous effects across industries with different levels of global value-chain integration.</p>
