My research interests are in applied economics, with a focus on issues relevant to Indonesia.

## Interests

- Macroeconomics and monetary policy
- International trade
- Inclusive growth and economic development
- Applied econometrics and policy evaluation

## Research experience

As a Research Assistant at the Bank Indonesia Institute (February 2023–May 2024), I supported research on macroeconomics, monetary economics, and public policy. My work included literature reviews, collecting and preparing economic data, and developing research summaries and presentations. I also supported policy seminars and capacity-building programmes.

My earlier research and data experience includes work on Indonesian household spending patterns and regional payment systems at Bank Indonesia, as well as data support for faculty research at Diponegoro University.

## Writing

I write research-informed analysis on Indonesian macroeconomics, economic development, institutions, and public policy. My writing connects economic arguments and empirical evidence to policy implications, including topics such as exchange-rate dynamics and inequality.
