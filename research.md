My interests are in applied macroeconomics and the economic policy challenges facing Indonesia. I am particularly interested in connecting economic theory with data and institutional detail.

## Research interests

- Macroeconomics and monetary policy
- International trade and exchange-rate dynamics
- Economic development, inequality, and inclusive growth
- Applied econometrics and policy evaluation

## Research and policy experience

### Bank Indonesia Institute
<span class="item-meta">Research Assistant · February 2023 – May 2024</span>

Supported research on macroeconomics, monetary economics, and public policy, including work related to sovereign wealth funds. Responsibilities included literature reviews, data collection and preparation, research materials, and support for seminars and staff training programmes.

### Bank Indonesia — Payment systems
<span class="item-meta">Data Analyst Intern · April – June 2021; August 2021 – January 2022</span>

Analysed payment transaction data related to household spending and consumer behaviour in Jakarta. Later supported data preparation and reporting on local-government transaction digitalisation and regional payment systems in Bali.
