I am an applied economist and **Master of Applied Economics graduate from the Australian National University (ANU)**. My interests lie in macroeconomics, monetary policy, international trade, economic development, and applied econometrics, particularly in the Indonesian context.

Before my master's studies, I worked as a **Research Assistant at Bank Indonesia Institute**, supporting research and policy-related analysis. I currently teach economics at ANU, and I am a founding member and writer at [Arthapala](https://www.arthapala.id/), where we publish research-informed essays on economic questions and public policy.

I use this website to share my research interests, writing, teaching experience, and academic materials. I am especially interested in making economic ideas and empirical evidence accessible without losing the underlying reasoning.
